\subsection*{Aggregated $\ARI$}
\phantomsection\label{app:ari_all}

\Cref{tab:app:ari_em,tab:app:ari_md} summarise mean per-window $\ARI$ for Feature \gls{dbscan}, joint Vanilla, Vanilla single-task, RoPE-TOA, and Bias.
Vanilla single-task is the matching Vanilla run of \cref{sec:exp:ablation}: emitter-only on the emitter table, mode-only on the mode table.
Feature \gls{dbscan} is the no-encoder control of \cref{tab:exp:vanilla}.
Joint rows are copied from \cref{tab:exp:attn_em,tab:exp:attn_md}, not from a second evaluation.
RoPE-az and RoPE-TOA+Bias remain in \cref{tab:exp:attn_em,tab:exp:attn_md} and are omitted here.

On the emitter branch, Vanilla single-task, RoPE-TOA, and Bias sit at the ceiling; joint Vanilla does not.
On the mode branch the neural runs occupy a narrow band, and Bias is highest.

\Cref{fig:app:hist_em_all,fig:app:hist_md_all} show the same four neural encoders on the log vertical axis of \cref{fig:exp:hist_em,fig:exp:hist_md}.
Vanilla single-task closes the emitter tail that joint Vanilla leaves; RoPE-TOA and Bias stay with it.
The mode curves rise later and closer together.
