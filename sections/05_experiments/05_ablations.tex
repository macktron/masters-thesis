\section{Ablation Studies}\label{sec:exp:ablation}

This section isolates the joint objective of \cref{eq:method:loss_total} from the attention variants of \cref{sec:exp:results:attention}.
The completed runs use the Vanilla encoder of \cref{sec:method:arch}, so the only change is which contrastive term is active.
That matched dual-branch control is the comparable transformer for RQ2: whether coupling deinterleaving with mode clustering outperforms training for one task alone.

The three objectives are:
\begin{description}
  \item[Joint] $\Loss = \Loss_{\mathrm{em}} + \lambda_{\mathrm{md}}\Loss_{\mathrm{md}}$ with $\lambda_{\mathrm{md}} = 1$, identical to Vanilla in \cref{sec:exp:results}.
  \item[Emitter-only] $\Loss = \Loss_{\mathrm{em}}$; the mode branch receives no direct gradient.
  \item[Mode-only] $\Loss = \Loss_{\mathrm{md}}$; the emitter branch receives no direct gradient.
\end{description}
Joint numbers in \cref{tab:exp:ablation_loss} are copied from the Vanilla row of \cref{tab:exp:attn_em,tab:exp:attn_md}, not from a second training run.
The single-task models are trained from the same initialisation seed, on the same data splits, and with the remaining hyperparameters of \cref{sec:exp:hparams}.
A mode-only Vanilla run is not yet included; its row will be filled when that checkpoint is evaluated.

Both branches are decoded with \gls{dbscan} in every run, including the branch that was not trained.
The informative cells are therefore the emitter scores of Mode-only and the mode scores of Emitter-only: they measure how much the shared trunk transfers to the unsupervised branch, versus collapse of that branch.
RQ2 is answered primarily by comparing Joint with Emitter-only on the emitter metrics; the mode column and the Mode-only row complete the symmetric picture.
