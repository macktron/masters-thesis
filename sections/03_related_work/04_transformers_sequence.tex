\section{Transformers for Time Series and Sets}\label{sec:related:transformers_seq}

The transformer encoder maps a sequence of tokens to contextualised representations by unmasked self-attention \parencite{vaswani2017attention,devlin2019bert}.
Every pulse in a window can attend to every other pulse, which matches the structure of interleaved \glspl{pdw}: the next pulse of the same emitter is generally not the next token.
The cost is quadratic in the window length, which is why \cref{sec:method:data:window} processes recordings in fixed-length windows.

Self-attention is permutation-equivariant.
Without a positional signal it treats the window as a set, which is the setting studied by the Set Transformer \parencite{lee2019set}.
Absolute sinusoidal encodings and learned position embeddings restore order for language, where the index of a token is a meaningful coordinate \parencite{vaswani2017attention}.
For \glspl{pdw} that coordinate is the wrong one.
Consecutive pulses are not equally spaced in time, so an ordinal position misrepresents \gls{toa} gaps.
Multivariate time-series transformers typically assume regular sampling and add a time-step index in the same way \parencite{zerveas2021timeseries}.
\textcite{gunn2025deinterleaving} therefore omit positional encodings and rely on \gls{toa} as an input feature.
The baseline encoder of \cref{sec:method:arch} makes the same choice.

Relative encodings offer a middle path.
\textcite{shaw2018relative} add a learned embedding of the discrete offset between token indices to the attention logits.
Graphormer generalises that idea to pairwise biases derived from graph distances \parencite{ying2021graphormer}.
The physical attention bias in \cref{sec:method:attention:bias} is the same mechanism with continuous pulse parameters: pairwise absolute differences of window-local \gls{toa} and of the two planar Euclidean incidence components, each scaled by a learned per-head coefficient and subtracted from the attention logits.

\textcite{su2024roformer} rotate the queries and keys (\cref{sec:bg:transformer}).
The inner product then depends on content and on the coordinate difference.
Vision models split the rotation planes across spatial axes \parencite{heo2024ropevit}.
The method replaces token indices with physical coordinates, reuses that split for the two Euclidean incidence components on the deinterleaving branch, and rotates the trunk and mode branch by \gls{toa} only (\cref{sec:method:attention:rope}).
It does not rotate by a scalar azimuth angle.

Existing positional schemes were designed for discrete indices, regular grids, or a single shared coordinate.
A \gls{pdw} window is an irregular point set with at least two physically distinct relations---time of arrival and arrival direction---and the two estimation tasks should not weight those relations equally.
Whether injecting them as a logit bias, as a rotary phase, or not at all improves clustering is taken up in \cref{ch:experiments}.
