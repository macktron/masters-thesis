\chapter{Related Work}\label{ch:related_work}

This chapter reviews work that the method in \cref{ch:method} builds on or differs from.
The first two sections cover radar-specific pipelines: classical sorting and identification, then learned classifiers and embedding models for \glspl{pdw}.
The remaining sections cover the learning ingredients used later---deep clustering, transformers on irregular sequences, and clustering at more than one granularity.
Each section ends with the limitation that the next chapter addresses.
