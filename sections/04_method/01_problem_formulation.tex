\section{Problem Formulation}\label{sec:method:formulation}

\subsection*{Input}

The data consist of $S$ recordings indexed by $s \in \{1,\ldots,S\}$.
The \emph{received pulse stream} of recording $s$ is a finite, time-ordered sequence of \glspl{pdw}
\[
  \mat{X}^{(s)} = (\vect{x}^{(s)}_1,\ldots,\vect{x}^{(s)}_{N_s}),
  \qquad
  \vect{x}^{(s)}_n \in \R^{d},
\]
where each $\vect{x}^{(s)}_n \in \R^{d}$ contains the measured parameters of one pulse (carrier frequency, amplitude, pulse width, time of arrival, and angles of arrival), and the length $N_s$ varies between recordings.
When we work with a single or arbitrary recording, we drop the superscript and simply write $\mat{X}$, $N$, and $\vect{x}_n$.

\subsection*{Labels and induced partitions}

Each pulse $n \in \{1,\ldots,N_s\}$ of recording $s$ carries two labels: an emitter identity $e^{(s)}_n \in \{1,\ldots,K^{(s)}_{\mathrm{em}}\}$ and an operating mode $m^{(s)}_n \in \{1,\ldots,M\}$.
The emitter count $K^{(s)}_{\mathrm{em}}$ is fixed per recording but varies between recordings. The mode symbol is drawn from a finite global catalogue of size $M$ that is shared across all recordings.
However, in real-world scenarios, the actual number of distinct modes present is not fixed or known in advance, and our architecture does not require $M$ as an input or impose a closed-set assumption.

For each recording these labels induce two partitions of the index set $\{1,\ldots,N_s\}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:method:emitter_partition}
  \mathcal{E}^{(s)}_k = \{\, n : e^{(s)}_n = k \,\},
  \qquad
  k \in \{1,\ldots,K^{(s)}_{\mathrm{em}}\},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:method:mode_partition}
  \mathcal{M}^{(s)}_\ell = \{\, n : m^{(s)}_n = \ell \,\},
  \qquad
  \ell \in \{1,\ldots,M\}.
\end{equation}

For each recording, the sets $\mathcal{E}^{(s)}_k$ are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive: $\mathcal{E}^{(s)}_k \cap \mathcal{E}^{(s)}_{k'} = \varnothing$ for $k \ne k'$, and $\bigcup_{k=1}^{K^{(s)}_{\mathrm{em}}} \mathcal{E}^{(s)}_k = \{1,\ldots,N_s\}$. Each cell is non-empty, since $K^{(s)}_{\mathrm{em}}$ counts only emitters actually present in the recording. In contrast, the sets $\mathcal{M}_\ell$ are mutually exclusive but not necessarily collectively exhaustive: a recording may only realize a subset of modes from the global catalogue $\{1,\ldots,M\}$, so $\mathcal{M}_\ell = \varnothing$ for any absent mode $\ell$. Mode coverage is thus not guaranteed or expected in recordings.

\subsection*{Dual embeddings and discrete outputs}

The parametric model maps $\mat{X}$ to two parallel embedding sequences,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:method:dual_embeddings}
  \mat{Z}^{\mathrm{em}} = (\vect{z}^{\mathrm{em}}_1,\ldots,\vect{z}^{\mathrm{em}}_N),
  \quad
  \vect{z}^{\mathrm{em}}_n \in \R^{p},
  \qquad
  \mat{Z}^{\mathrm{md}} = (\vect{z}^{\mathrm{md}}_1,\ldots,\vect{z}^{\mathrm{md}}_N),
  \quad
  \vect{z}^{\mathrm{md}}_n \in \R^{q}.
\end{equation}
The emitter branch $\mat{Z}^{\mathrm{em}}$ is trained so that pulses sharing an emitter are close in $\R^{p}$ and pulses with distinct emitters are pushed apart, and the mode branch $\mat{Z}^{\mathrm{md}}$ is trained analogously against $\{m_n\}$.
Both branches use the same batch-wide aggregation; the only asymmetry between them is the semantics of their labels, which propagates to the positive sets the loss reads (\cref{sec:method:loss}).

Discrete per-pulse outputs
\begin{equation}\label{eq:method:emitter_predictor}
  \hat{e}_n \in \{1,\ldots,\hat{K}_{\mathrm{em}}\},
  \qquad n \in \{1,\ldots,N\},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:method:mode_predictor}
  \hat{m}_n \in \{1,\ldots,\hat{M}\},
  \qquad n \in \{1,\ldots,N\},
\end{equation}
are obtained \emph{post hoc} by clustering $\{\vect{z}^{\mathrm{em}}_n\}$ within each stream and $\{\vect{z}^{\mathrm{md}}_n\}$ across streams.
The cardinalities $\hat{K}_{\mathrm{em}}$ and $\hat{M}$ are determined by the clustering rather than fixed in advance; in particular $\hat{M}$ may exceed $M$ when modulation types absent from training are encountered at inference.

\subsection*{Learning objective}

Given a labelled collection of received pulse streams, the goal is to fit the parameters of the map $\mat{X} \mapsto (\mat{Z}^{\mathrm{em}}, \mat{Z}^{\mathrm{md}})$ by minimising a sum of two supervised metric-learning losses that share their functional form and a common batch-wide aggregation, differing only in which label drives their positive sets: emitter identifiers on $\mat{Z}^{\mathrm{em}}$ and mode labels on $\mat{Z}^{\mathrm{md}}$.
On a previously unseen stream, the inference task is to recover the partitions of \cref{eq:method:emitter_partition,eq:method:mode_partition} up to relabelling of the cluster symbols.
