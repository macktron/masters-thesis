\section{Thesis Outline}\label{sec:intro:outline}

\Cref{ch:background} covers pulsed radar, \glspl{pdw}, emitters and modes, the \gls{elint} chain, cluster agreement scores, and the machine-learning and transformer background used later.
\Cref{ch:related_work} surveys classical emitter identification, supervised radar models, deep clustering, sequence transformers, and joint clustering, and states the gap that motivates the method.
\Cref{ch:method} defines the problem, the preprocessing, the encoder and losses, the attention variants, training, the choice of scores, and baselines.
\Cref{ch:experiments} reports setup, the synthetic corpus, hyperparameters, joint Vanilla scores, objective ablations, attention variants, and qualitative plots.
\Cref{ch:discussion} interprets the results, compares them with prior work, and states limitations.
\Cref{ch:conclusion} answers the research questions and lists future work.
